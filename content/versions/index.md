# Version differences

*What changed between the DOS, Windows and Macintosh releases of Colonization, and what stayed the same.*

Sid Meier's Colonization shipped for DOS in 1994 and was then ported to
Windows 3.1 (1995) and the Macintosh. The rules carried over largely intact; the presentation was
rebuilt for each platform.
